**Full description**

Your collection is an asset — track it like one. SportsTicker turns any sports card search into a live price ticker right from your toolbar.

Search any card — Michael Jordan rookies, Lebron prizms, Tom Brady, Shohei Ohtani, Wayne Gretzky — and add it to your watchlist. Each tracked search shows its median price across eBay's active fixed-price listings, with the listing count, so outliers never distort the number. Every entry keeps its own price history in your browser, giving you real trend arrows (▲/▼ since your last check) and a mini sparkline of recent moves. A scrolling ticker tape across the top of the popup keeps your whole watchlist moving in real time, and it auto-refreshes while the popup is open. Click any card to jump straight to its live eBay search.

Setting up is quick: paste a free eBay developer access token once (about two minutes at developer.ebay.com, no payment details), hit Test token, and you're trading. Tokens expire after a couple of hours — if eBay ever rejects one, the popup points you straight to the settings to fix it.

It all lives in a small popup — no new tab, no clutter. If the feed drops, your last prices stay on screen with how old they are, color-coded from green (fresh) to red (long offline). Your watchlist and token are stored locally, and nothing is tracked.

Free to use forever. If the ticker pays for itself, a coffee is always appreciated. Found something broken? Send a quick note to contactae2000@gmail.com.
