SportsTicker

Connect eBay to price cards

Card prices come from eBay's Browse API. It needs a free developer access token — about two minutes to get one:

  1. Create a free app at developer.ebay.com
  2. Open the OAuth token generator, pick your app, and copy the access token
  3. Paste it below — tokens expire after about 2 hours, so re-paste anytime
