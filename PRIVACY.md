# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

A hosted copy of this policy is available at https://mrfentmen.github.io/privacy-policies/sports-card-ticker.html

SportsTicker is a free extension. It is built to be private by default.

## What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The extension:

- Stores your watchlist (the card searches you track and the last prices you saw) locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data never leaves your device.
- Stores the eBay access token you paste into the ⚙ settings locally in your browser. It is sent only to eBay's API (api.ebay.com) when the extension fetches prices — the same way any request from your browser to eBay works.
- Makes network requests to eBay's public Browse API (api.ebay.com) to search listings and fetch current prices. The requests carry the token you provided and a descriptive User-Agent string identifying the extension. No account information or personal data is sent.

## What we do not collect

- No personal information, names, emails, or addresses
- No health, financial, or payment information
- No authentication credentials beyond the token you voluntarily paste for eBay
- No communications
- No location data
- No web history or browsing activity
- No clicks, keystrokes, or activity tracking

## Sharing

We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use user data for any purpose other than running the extension's single purpose of showing sports card prices from eBay.
