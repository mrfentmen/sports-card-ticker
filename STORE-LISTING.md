## Detailed description

Sports cards are the new stock market — track them like one. SportsTicker searches eBay's live marketplace for any card query ("michael jordan rookie card", "tom brady prizm", "lebron james topps") and shows the median fixed-price listing value as a clean ticker in your toolbar. Refreshes every 60 seconds.

**One-time setup (2 minutes):** SportsTicker needs a free eBay developer access token. Create an app at developer.ebay.com, copy the token from the OAuth token generator, and paste it into the ⚙ settings — that's it. Tokens expire every ~2 hours so re-paste anytime. The extension walks you through it.

**What you can do:**

- **Search any card query** — the more precise your search, the sharper the median. "1986 fleer michael jordan #57 psa 8" works great
- **Median market price** — pulls up to 40 fixed-price eBay listings and computes the median, giving you a real market snapshot (not just whatever the top listing happens to be)
- **Price trends at a glance** — trend arrows from client-side history, 24h change, ATH/ATL range, and 7-day volatility %
- **30-day sparkline** — a mini price chart on every card row, hover for a 7-day breakdown with dates
- **Price alerts** — set alert-above, alert-below, or percentage-based thresholds; fires a chime when crossed
- **24-hour alert log** — review every alert that fired across all your searches
- **Sort & organize** — sort by price, gainers/losers, name, or added order; ⭐ pin favorites to the top; drag to reorder
- **Right-click menu** — copy a price or name, remove a search instantly
- **Keyboard navigation** — Arrow keys to move through cards, Enter to open the eBay listing, Delete to remove
- **Dark/light theme** — toggle between modes, persisted between sessions
- **Export & copy** — download your watchlist as CSV, or copy all prices to clipboard in one click
- **Compact view** — shrink rows to fit more cards on screen
- **Snooze alerts** — mute all alerts for an hour with one click, countdown shown
- **Undo remove** — accidentally deleted a search? Undo link stays for 4 seconds
- **Refresh countdown ring** — see when the next 60s auto-refresh will happen
- **Ctrl/Cmd+R** — instant manual refresh
- **Ctrl/Cmd+K** — jump to search instantly
- **Ctrl/Cmd+Plus/Minus/0** — zoom the popup
- **Offline resilience** — shows your last good prices with a color-coded staliness indicator when eBay is unreachable

**Private by design.** Your watchlist, favorites, price history, alert thresholds, and eBay token live entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere except the eBay API calls the extension makes on your behalf with your own token.

**eBay token required.** Get a free token in two minutes at developer.ebay.com — the extension's ⚙ settings page walks you through every step.
